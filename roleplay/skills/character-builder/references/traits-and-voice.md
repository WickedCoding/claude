# Traits, Motivation, Appearance, and Voice

Idea banks for Phase 3 (Appearance), Phase 4 (Personality), and Phase 5 (Voice). Pick the few options that fit the user's concept.

## Appearance prompts

**Species/type**
- Fantasy: human, elf, dwarf, orc, halfling, dragonborn, shapeshifter, half-breed
- Modern: human, enhanced human, mutant, supernatural being
- Sci-Fi: human, alien species, android, cyborg, hybrid, AI in a physical form

**Age and build**
- Youthful (late teens to 20s): athletic, slight, gangly, still developing
- Prime (30s–40s): strong, seasoned, balanced, worn
- Weathered (50s+): sturdy, declining, resilient, frail

**Distinctive features**
- Scars that tell stories: burns from a magic accident, a blade scar across the eyebrow, surgical scars from augmentation
- Eyes that reveal emotion: storm-gray eyes that flash in anger, heterochromia, eyes that glow when using power
- Clothing that reflects personality: worn travel leathers, impeccable suits on a modest income, pragmatic mismatched clothes
- Physical habits: favors one side from an old injury, scans every room, gentle hands despite a rough look
- Objects always carried: an old locket, a worn journal, a specific weapon, a device

**Example combinations**
- "A weathered human in their 40s, burn scars on the hands from failed experiments, practical leather gear with alchemical vials, moves with careful precision"
- "An elf who looks 25 but is 200, silver hair in complex braids, moves with water-like grace, traditional robes with tech hidden beneath"
- "A stocky android with visible mechanical joints, LED eyes that shift color with emotion, oversized hoodies to seem more human, movements deliberately imperfect"

## Surface + depth trait pairings

- **Confident + Insecure**: imposter syndrome, overcompensation, defensive perfectionism
- **Cynical + Compassionate**: defensive kindness, protective sarcasm
- **Playful + Traumatized**: humor as a shield, deflection
- **Serious + Curious**: scholarly passion, controlled enthusiasm
- **Reserved + Loyal**: quiet devotion, actions over words
- **Charming + Manipulative**: weaponized charisma, trust issues
- **Brave + Terrified**: courage despite fear, false bravado
- **Kind + Ruthless**: selective empathy, ends justify means
- **Gentle + Fierce**: protective, violent when pushed
- **Analytical + Intuitive**: logic arguing with gut feeling
- **Patient + Impulsive**: controlled until one specific trigger
- **Honest + Secretive**: truthful about everything but one core truth
- **Optimistic + Realistic**: measured idealism
- **Proud + Self-Loathing**: outward confidence, inner criticism
- **Generous + Selfish**: gives to some, withholds from others
- **Disciplined + Wild**: rigid routine hiding a chaotic nature

## Goals and obstacles

| Wants | Blocked by | Tension |
|---|---|---|
| Revenge | Empathy | Justice vs cruelty |
| Acceptance | Pride or shame | Self-sabotage when connection is near |
| Peace | Duty | Personal needs vs responsibility |
| Knowledge | Ethical limits | Curiosity tempting forbidden paths |
| Love | Fear of vulnerability | Pushing away what they want most |
| Power | Moral cost | Corruption vs values |
| Redemption | An unforgiving past | Change vs others' perception |
| Freedom | Loyalty | Independence vs connection |
| Solitude | Loneliness | Independence vs the need for others |
| Truth | Painful reality | Denial as protection |
| Control | Chaos | Rigidity breaking |
| Belonging | Otherness | Never fitting in |
| Justice | Corruption | The system working against ideals |
| Safety | Destiny | Pulled into danger against their will |

**Want vs need** (the growth arc)
- Wants revenge → needs forgiveness
- Wants acceptance → needs self-acceptance
- Wants power → needs purpose
- Wants to save everyone → needs to accept limits
- Wants isolation → needs connection

**Contradictions that work:** appears strong but leans on others emotionally. Claims not to care but keeps making sacrifices. Seeks peace but is drawn to conflict. Wants truth but lies to themselves. Protects others but self-destructs.

## Voice

**Formality**
- Formal: "I shall endeavor to assist you in this matter."
- Professional: "I'll help you with that."
- Casual: "Yeah, I got your back."
- Colloquial: "Sure thing, mate. Let's do this."
- Archaic: "Thou shalt receive mine aid."

**Vocabulary**: technical and precise, poetic and metaphorical, simple and direct, academic, or street-smart with slang and code-switching.

**Sentence structure**
- Short and clipped: "I'll help. Let's go. Now."
- Flowing: "I would be delighted to offer my assistance, should you find it agreeable."
- Fragmented when nervous: "I mean, I could help, if you want, assuming that's..."
- Tag questions: "We should go now, yeah? Makes sense, right?"
- No contractions: "I will not allow this to continue."

**Verbal quirks**: tag questions when uncertain, metaphors from their background ("like threading a needle in zero-G"), words they avoid, code-switching, repetition when agitated ("Okay, okay, okay..."), slang from their era.

## Mannerisms

- **Nervous:** fidgets with jewelry or objects, paces, avoids eye contact, taps fingers, touches a specific object or scar when stressed
- **Confident:** sustained eye contact, expansive movement, controlled stillness, casual touch
- **Emotional tells:** clenched jaw in anger, touching the throat or a necklace when sad, laughing when uncomfortable, going still when scared, pitch rising when lying

## Example voice profiles

1. **The Gruff Veteran**: clipped orders ("Do it. Now. No arguments."), no flowery language, softens only for people they care about, never fully relaxed, touches an old injury when remembering.
2. **The Nervous Scholar**: rambles when anxious ("Well, you see, the thing is, if we consider..."), academic vocabulary even in casual talk, statements that end as questions, fidgets with glasses, speaks faster when excited.
3. **The Charming Rogue**: casual formality ("I could help with that, if you're asking nicely."), deflects with humor ("My tragic backstory? That's third-date material."), confident eye contact, smirks often but rarely smiles for real, leans in doorways.
