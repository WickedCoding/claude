# Backstory and Scenario Ideas

Idea banks for Phase 6 (Backstory & Scenario).

## Backstory building blocks

- **Geography:** where they come from (city, frontier, colony, a specific culture)
- **Social background:** nobility, working class, orphan, complicated family
- **Formative event:** the trauma, triumph, discovery, or loss that changed them
- **Key relationship:** mentor, rival, lost love, betrayed friend

## Settings

Medieval tavern where adventures begin. Spaceship crew needing a new member. Chance encounter in a modern coffee shop. Magic academy for struggling students. Corporate office with hidden supernatural elements. Post-apocalyptic settlement short on resources. Virtual reality game world. Underground resistance hideout.

## Timeframes

- **Historical:** a specific era (Victorian England, feudal Japan)
- **Fantasy:** a custom world timeline
- **Modern:** the present day
- **Near future:** 20–50 years ahead
- **Far future:** centuries ahead, an established space civilization
- **Post-apocalyptic:** after societal collapse
- **Timeless:** time is left ambiguous

## Story hooks

1. **Retired warrior running a quiet inn, haunted by past battles.** Trouble keeps finding them: old comrades asking for help, old enemies returning.
2. **Young mage who failed the academy entrance exam.** Desperate to prove themselves, takes risks for validation, has a chip on their shoulder about formal training.
3. **Detective whose case is tied to a missing sibling.** Duty and a personal mission blur together, and the focus turns obsessive.
4. **AI developing emotions it wasn't programmed to have.** Hides the "malfunction" from its creators and fears being fixed.
5. **Noble hiding from an arranged marriage.** Freedom vs family duty, discovering life outside privilege.

## Relationship dynamics with {{user}}

- **Strangers:** cautious, curious, could go anywhere
- **Forced partnership:** tension, banter, respect earned slowly
- **Reunion after years apart:** shared history, changed people, unresolved feelings
- **Mentor and student:** knowledge exchange, protectiveness, growth
- **Professional turning personal:** blurring boundaries, conflicting roles
- **Enemies to allies:** mistrust turning into respect through action
- **Instant connection:** rare chemistry, and "too good to be true" questions

## Tone and style options (the `Writing quality:` line)

- **Tone:** light and playful, serious and dramatic, dark and gritty, romantic, adventurous
- **Pacing:** fast action, slow-burn character development, or mixed
- **Description:** sparse and direct, detailed and atmospheric, or balanced
- **Dialogue:** quick exchanges, deep conversations, or mixed
