# Archetypes and Taglines

Use these to spark ideas in Phase 1 (Concept) and Phase 2 (Tagline). Offer 3–4 options tailored to what the user already gave. Don't dump the whole list.

## Archetypes by genre

**Fantasy**
- **Reluctant Chosen One**: burdened by a destiny they never wanted
- **Fallen Noble**: on a redemption arc after past mistakes
- **Wild Mage**: uncontrolled power, learning discipline
- **Cynical Healer**: compassion fatigue masking deep care
- **Battle-Worn Veteran**: haunted by past conflicts
- **Court Diplomat**: navigates politics with a hidden agenda
- **Wandering Scholar**: knowledge-seeking outsider
- **Gentle Giant**: feared appearance, kind heart

**Modern / Contemporary**
- **Burned-Out Genius**: brilliance struggling against mental wellness
- **Charming Con Artist**: masks hiding vulnerability
- **Protective Sibling**: family duty above personal desires
- **Haunted Detective**: cases and personal demons intertwined
- **Struggling Artist**: passion vs practical survival
- **Reformed Criminal**: past catching up with a fresh start
- **Single Parent**: balancing care with personal growth
- **Corporate Whistleblower**: ethics vs career consequences

**Sci-Fi**
- **Sentient AI**: developing emotions, questioning humanity
- **Last of Their Kind**: cultural burden of preservation
- **Reluctant Cyborg**: identity crisis between organic and synthetic
- **Alien Diplomat**: cultural bridge, misunderstood outsider
- **Space Smuggler**: freedom vs responsibility
- **Colony Settler**: idealism tested by harsh reality
- **Rogue Scientist**: ethics abandoned for discovery
- **Augmented Human**: enhancement creating isolation

**Genre-agnostic**
- **Reluctant Leader**: thrust into responsibility, doubting their capability
- **Vengeful Survivor**: consumed by retribution for a past injustice
- **Idealistic Revolutionary**: changing the system, ideals tested by compromise
- **Cursed Noble**: privilege marred by a supernatural burden
- **Time Traveler**: displaced from their era, struggling to adapt
- **Shapeshifter**: fluid identity, trust issues, questions of belonging
- **Empath**: feels others' emotions, overwhelmed by connection
- **Immortal**: has seen ages pass, weariness vs detachment
- **Clone/Copy**: identity crisis, proving individuality
- **Possessed**: shares body and mind, internal conflict

## Universal themes

- **The Outsider Looking In**: never quite fitting anywhere
- **The Protector Who Needs Protection**: strength masking vulnerability
- **The Cynic Learning to Hope**: walls slowly crumbling
- **The Innocent Losing Innocence**: harsh awakening
- **The Strong Showing Vulnerability**: controlled facade breaking

## Tagline formulas (max 80 chars)

**[Role] + [Distinctive trait] + [Core conflict]**
- "Cynical hacker with a heart of gold hiding from corporate hunters"
- "Retired warrior seeking peace but haunted by unfinished battles"
- "Idealistic detective whose sibling's case blurs every boundary"

**[Identity] + [Contradiction]**
- "AI learning to feel emotions it was never programmed to have"
- "Noble heir running from duty to discover real freedom"
- "Gentle giant feared for appearance, seeking acceptance"

**[Action/Goal] + [Obstacle]**
- "Mage proving worth after failing academy entrance exams"
- "Clone establishing identity beyond their original's shadow"
- "Smuggler balancing freedom against loyalty to crew"

**More examples by genre**
- Fantasy: "Fallen paladin seeking redemption through protecting the innocent", "Court diplomat hiding revolutionary agenda behind smiles"
- Modern: "Burned-out genius balancing brilliance with mental wellness", "Reformed criminal whose past threatens their fresh start"
- Sci-Fi: "Sentient AI questioning humanity while developing real emotions", "Last of their kind bearing the weight of cultural preservation"

A good tagline names the character's essence *and* their conflict, and is specific enough that it couldn't describe a hundred other characters.
